HOW TO PLAY

One rule changes everything.

① PLACE

You play X. The computer plays O. Take turns placing marks on the 3×3 grid.

② THREE MARKS

Each player can normally hold only three active marks on the board at once.

③ MAKE A LINE

Three of your marks in a row — horizontal, vertical or diagonal — wins instantly.

④ THE ∞ RULE

Place a fourth mark without winning, and your oldest mark disappears.

⑤ IMPORTANT

The win is always checked BEFORE the oldest mark is removed. A winning fourth move never gets undone.
